  • Delivery is clean. 145 workflow emails went out, 145 delivered, 0 bounced. The student domain is healthy.

⛔  What didn't
  • Nothing converted. 145 emails → 0 form submissions, 0 appointments, 0 pipeline stage moves. In 7 days the student email machine produced nothing we can measure.

  • 338 of 367 pipeline leads (92%) have never left "New Lead." A paused January campaign is still holding 1,227 queued contacts — un-pause it by accident and 1,227 people get a February email in September.

  • Delete the paused "Feb 4th" campaign.
