## What worked
- Delivery is clean. 145 workflow emails went out, 145 delivered, 0 bounced. The student domain is healthy.
- Volume is steady: 21 subject lines running through the nurture workflows. "You already have the obsession" (35 sends) and "The 4-week AI implementation plan" (27) carry most of it.

## What didn't
- Nothing converted. 145 emails → 0 form submissions, 0 appointments, 0 pipeline stage moves. In 7 days the student email machine produced nothing we can measure.
- We're blind on opens and clicks. GHL's per-message status only ever says "delivered." The real open/click numbers sit behind a stats endpoint our token can't read (401 = it exists, wrong scope). So we can't tell if the problem is subject lines (nobody opens) or content (they open, don't click).
- No email gets credit for anything. Purchases and bookings only tie back to an email when the link carries utm_source=email. Ours didn't. Fixed going forward.
- 338 of 367 pipeline leads (92%) have never left "New Lead." A paused January campaign is still holding 1,227 queued contacts — un-pause it by accident and 1,227 people get a February email in September.

## Change this week
- Get the email-stats scope on the GHL token. Until then every report is a delivery report, not a performance report.
- Delete the paused "Feb 4th" campaign.
