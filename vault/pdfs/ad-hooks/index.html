Pain hooks

01

Name the problem before you name the product. People stop for a problem they recognize.

  1. Still dealing with [PROBLEM] every [WEEK / MONTH]? There's an easier way.
  2. The real reason [PROBLEM] keeps coming back. It's not what most people think.
  3. Most [AUDIENCE] make this mistake with [TOPIC]. Here's how to avoid it.
  4. Stop paying for [EXPENSIVE FIX] before you try this.
  5. What nobody tells you about [TOPIC] until it's too late.
  6. That small [ISSUE] turns into a [BIG, COSTLY PROBLEM] faster than you'd think.
  7. Sick of [FRUSTRATION]? So were our customers. So we fixed it.
  8. If [SITUATION] just happened, don't [COMMON MISTAKE] yet. Read this first.
  9. The [SEASON] problem every [AUDIENCE] in [CITY] runs into.
  10. You shouldn't have to [ANNOYING TASK] to get [BASIC RESULT].
TipUse the exact words customers use when they call you. "My AC is blowing warm air" beats "HVAC performance issues."

Result hooks

02

Show the after. People buy the outcome, not the service.

  1. How a [CUSTOMER TYPE] went from [BEFORE] to [AFTER] in [TIME].Use a real customer story only.
  2. Imagine [DESIRED OUTCOME] by [DATE OR EVENT].
  3. [RESULT] without [THING THEY HATE].
  4. Here's what [OUTCOME] actually looks like for a [AUDIENCE].
  5. The [NUMBER]-minute [ACTION] that [RESULT].
  6. Get [RESULT] this [WEEK / MONTH], even if [COMMON OBJECTION].
  7. This is exactly what [PRICE] gets you at [BUSINESS].
  8. Your [THING], but [CLEANER / FASTER / CHEAPER TO RUN].
  9. Done in [TIME]. Built to last [TIME].
  10. Picture your [SPACE / PROJECT] like this before [HOLIDAY / EVENT].
TipPair a result hook with a real photo of your own work. Stock photos make good hooks look fake.

Curiosity hooks

03

Open a loop the reader wants closed. Then actually close it in the ad. No bait and switch.

  1. I almost didn't post this.
  2. We don't usually share this, but [INSIDER TIP].
  3. The [TOPIC] question we get asked most. The answer surprises people.
  4. 3 things I'd never do as a [YOUR PROFESSION].
  5. Watch what happens when we [ACTION].
  6. One change that made the biggest difference for our [CUSTOMERS].
  7. [CITY], you need to see this.
  8. Why we stopped [COMMON INDUSTRY PRACTICE].
  9. The [ITEM] most people throw away that could [BENEFIT].
  10. Most people [DO COMMON THING] the hard way. Here's the easy way.
TipCuriosity hooks work best in video. Say the hook in the first 2 seconds and put it on screen as text too.

Proof hooks

04

Let your customers do the selling. Every number and quote here must be real.

  1. "[SHORT REAL REVIEW QUOTE]" – [FIRST NAME], [CITY]
  2. [REAL NUMBER] [CITY] families trusted us with [SERVICE] this year.
  3. Before and after: [PROJECT] in [NEIGHBORHOOD].Fine for homes, cars and spaces. Avoid before/after for health, weight or appearance.
  4. We've done [REAL NUMBER] [JOBS]. Here's what we've learned.
  5. Rated [RATING] on Google by [NUMBER] neighbors. Here's why.
  6. Why [CUSTOMER TYPE] keep coming back to [BUSINESS].
  7. A real message we got from a customer this week:
  8. Licensed, insured and serving [CITY] since [YEAR].
  9. What [NUMBER] customers said about [SERVICE]. Unfiltered.
  10. Our most requested [SERVICE], and why people keep asking for it.
TipA screenshot of a real text or review as the ad image, with hook 37 as the first line, is one of the simplest ads to make.

Offer & local hooks

05

Lead with the deal when you have a good one. Deadlines and limits must be real.

  1. [CITY]: [OFFER] this [MONTH] only.
  2. Free [BONUS] for the first [REAL NUMBER] people who book.
  3. Now booking [MONTH]. [REAL NUMBER] spots left.
  4. [PRICE] [SERVICE]. No upsells. No surprises.
  5. New to [CITY]? Here's [OFFER] to welcome you.
  6. Free download: the [SEASON] [TOPIC] checklist.
  7. Take the 60-second quiz: is your [THING] [COSTING YOU MONEY / READY FOR WINTER]?
  8. Black Friday at [BUSINESS]: [REAL OFFER], [START DATE] to [END DATE].
  9. Comment "[KEYWORD]" and we'll send you [FREE THING].
  10. Locals only: [OFFER] for [NEIGHBORHOOD] residents.
TipHook 49 works great for lead magnets. Set up an automatic DM reply so nobody waits for the free thing.

Bonus prompts

+3

Copy these into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Replace everything in [BRACKETS] first.

1. Write 30 hooks for your business

You'll get 30 hooks across all 5 angles, ranked strongest first.

You are a direct-response copywriter who has written Facebook ads for local businesses for 10 years.

My business: [BUSINESS + CITY]
What I'm promoting: [OFFER + PRICE]
Who it's for: [AUDIENCE]
What they're frustrated about: [MAIN PROBLEM]

Write 30 ad hooks, 6 for each angle: pain, result, curiosity, proof, offer. Each under 12 words. Then rank the top 5 and say why.

Rules: use only details I gave you. Don't invent numbers or reviews. Talk about the situation, not the person's traits. No em dashes, no emojis, no hype words.

2. Turn your best hook into a full ad

You'll get 3 complete ads built on your hook, ready to paste into Meta.

You are a Facebook ads copywriter for small businesses.

Hook: [YOUR HOOK]
Business and offer: [BUSINESS, OFFER, PRICE]
Proof I have (real only): [REVIEW, NUMBER OF JOBS, YEARS IN BUSINESS]

Write 3 ads that start with this hook. For each: primary text (60 to 100 words), headline (under 40 characters), and one call to action. Version A short, B story-style, C bullet points. Then tell me which to run first.

Rules: problem before offer, one clear next step, write like a person talking to one person.
