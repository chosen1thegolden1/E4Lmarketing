# E4L Vault — Skills build spec (launch)

Six skills ship at launch. Each one works in **both Claude and ChatGPT**.

| Skill | Job | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|
| Ad Writer | Facebook, Instagram and Google ads, hooks and variations | Ads prompts (AD-xx) |
| Email Writer | Sequences, sales emails, texts, newsletters | Email prompts (EM-xx) |
| Content Planner | Calendars, posts, carousels, video scripts | Content prompts (CT-xx) |
| Offer Builder | Offers, pricing tiers, landing pages, objection scripts | Offers prompts (OF-xx) |
| Clip Finder | Picks the best moments from a transcript, cut list, captions | Video editing series |
| Results Reader | Reads ad, email and funnel numbers; says what to change | Analytics prompts (AN-xx) |

## Requirements for every skill
1. **Shared SKILL.md format.** Claude and ChatGPT both use it, so one folder installs in either app.
2. **60-second setup interview** on first use: business, offers, customers, city, voice (with a pasted writing sample). The skill reuses these answers instead of asking every time.
3. **Project version.** A copy-paste version of the same instructions for ChatGPT plans that don't have Skills yet (ChatGPT is still rolling Skills out by plan), and for Claude Projects.
4. **Follows `STYLE.md`**: honest claims only, banned-word list, no invented numbers or reviews.
5. **Tested in both apps** on at least two real business examples before release. Re-checked when either app ships a major update.
6. **One 5-minute tutorial** showing install in Claude and in ChatGPT, plus the Project fallback.
7. **Client-use license** applies, same as the rest of the Vault.

## Notes
- ChatGPT plan availability for Skills varies and has changed during 2026. Confirm the current rollout before recording tutorials, and keep the sales-page wording ("if your ChatGPT plan doesn't have Skills yet…") until it's available on every plan.
