# Content & Social

These prompts help you plan, write and fix your content so you post with a plan instead of guessing. Fill in the blanks in square brackets, paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and edit the result so it sounds like you.

---

### CT-01 Build a 30-day content calendar for my business

**Use it when:** You want a month of posts planned before you sit down to write any of them.
**You'll get:** A 30-day calendar table with a topic, format, hook and goal for every post.
**Works in:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

```text
You are a social media strategist who plans monthly content for small local businesses and solo service providers.

Build me a 30-day content calendar.

My business: [BUSINESS / e.g. mobile dog grooming in Tampa]
Who I serve: [AUDIENCE]
Main offer this month: [OFFER]
Platforms I post on: [PLATFORMS / e.g. Instagram, Facebook]
Posts per week I can realistically make: [NUMBER]

Before you write anything, ask me up to 5 questions you need answered. Wait for my answers.

Then give me:
1. A one-line goal for the month.
2. 4 content pillars (themes) with one sentence on why each matters to my customers.
3. A table with columns: Day, Pillar, Format (reel, carousel, photo post, story, text post), Topic, Hook (first line, under 12 words), Goal (reach, trust or sales).
4. A mix of roughly 40% helpful, 30% trust-building, 20% personal or behind the scenes, 10% direct offer.
5. Two "low energy" days per week marked with an easy format I can make in under 15 minutes.

Rules:
- Talk about the customer's problem before the offer.
- Use specific details from what I gave you. Don't invent statistics or results.
- Write like a person talking to one person. No jargon.
- Never use these words: delve, leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, robust.
- No em dashes. No hashtags. No emojis.
```

**Pro tip:** After you get the table, send "Write the first 7 posts in full" to turn week one into finished content.

### CT-02 Find 25 content ideas my customers actually care about

**Use it when:** You are stuck on what to post and want ideas based on real customer questions, not trends.
**You'll get:** 25 content ideas grouped by the buyer's stage, each with a hook and a format.
**Works in:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

```text
You are a content strategist who interviews small business customers to find out what they worry about before, during and after they buy.

My business: [BUSINESS]
Who I serve: [AUDIENCE]
What I sell: [OFFER / e.g. kitchen remodels from $25,000]
Questions customers ask me most: [PASTE 3 TO 10 REAL QUESTIONS, if you have them]
Objections I hear: [OBJECTIONS / e.g. "too expensive", "I'll do it myself"]

Give me 25 content ideas in 5 groups of 5:
1. Before they know they need me (problems they notice)
2. While they compare options (questions and fears)
3. Right before they buy (objections, price, timing)
4. After they buy (how to get the most from it)
5. Personal and local (me, my team, my area)

For each idea give: a working title, a first-line hook under 12 words, the best format (short video, carousel, photo post, text post), and one sentence on why this customer would stop scrolling for it.

Rules:
- Base ideas on real problems and questions, not generic tips anyone could post.
- Use the details I gave you. Don't invent statistics or customer stories.
- Write like a person talking to one person.
- Never use these words: delve, leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, robust.
- No em dashes. No hashtags. No emojis.

Then tell me the 3 ideas you'd post first and why.
```

**Pro tip:** Paste in real questions from your DMs, emails or reviews and the ideas get much more specific.

### CT-03 Write 5 short vertical video scripts with hooks and shot lists

**Use it when:** You want to post Reels, TikToks or Shorts but freeze when you hit record.
**You'll get:** 5 scripts of 30 to 45 seconds, each with 3 hook options, spoken lines, a shot list and on-screen text.
**Works in:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

```text
You are a short-form video producer who scripts Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts for small business owners who film on their phone.

My business: [BUSINESS]
Who I serve: [AUDIENCE]
Topic or goal for these videos: [TOPIC / e.g. why cheap roof repairs cost more later]
Where I can film: [LOCATION / e.g. my shop, job sites, my car]
Am I comfortable talking to camera? [YES / NO / ONLY VOICEOVER]

Write 5 different video scripts. Each one runs 30 to 45 seconds (about 75 to 110 spoken words). For each give:
1. Three hook options for the first 2 seconds (spoken line under 10 words plus what is on screen).
2. The full script split into numbered beats, with a timestamp for each beat.
3. A shot list: what the camera shows during each beat, filmable on a phone with no crew.
4. On-screen text for each beat, 6 words or fewer.
5. One closing line that tells viewers what to do next (follow, comment a word, book, visit).
6. A caption of 1 to 3 sentences.

Use 5 different styles: myth vs truth, quick tutorial, mistake to avoid, day in the life, customer question answered.

Rules:
- Talk about the viewer's problem before the offer.
- Write like a person talking out loud. Short sentences. No jargon.
- Don't invent statistics or results.
- Never use these words: delve, leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, robust.
- No em dashes. No hashtags. No emojis.
```

**Pro tip:** Read each script out loud once before filming and cut any word you would not say to a customer in person.
