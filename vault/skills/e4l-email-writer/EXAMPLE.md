# E4L Email Writer: example run

A short example with Bright Smile Dental, so you can see what a run looks like. Long parts are cut with "...".

**Member:** I miss calls all day when I'm with patients. Write me a text that goes out when I can't pick up.

**Email Writer** (Business Card: Bright Smile Dental, Tulsa, new patient cleaning and exam $99, Dr. Reyes):

> Job 6, missed-call texts. Here are 3 versions. Each is under 160 characters.
>
> **1. Straight to booking**
> Hi, it's Bright Smile Dental. Sorry we missed you, we're with a patient. Want to grab a time? Book here: [BOOKING LINK] or reply with a good time to call.
>
> **2. Question first**
> Hi, this is Bright Smile Dental. Sorry we missed your call! What can we help with? Reply here and we'll get right back to you.
>
> **3. New patient offer**
> Bright Smile Dental here. Missed your call. New patients: cleaning and exam is $99. Reply YES and we'll text you open times this week.
>
> Use version 2 first. A question gets more replies than a link, and the reply tells you what they need.
>
> Next step: want the follow-up text for people who don't answer within 2 hours?

*Made-up business for illustration. Your texts will use your own Business Card.*
