# E4L Offer Builder: example run

A short example with Sparkle Home Cleaning, so you can see what a run looks like. Long parts are cut with "...".

**Member:** Find the holes in my offer. "Deep clean, $250, book online."

**Offer Builder** (Business Card: Sparkle Home Cleaning, Charlotte, 2 cleaners, deep clean $250, regular clean $140):

> Job 14. Here's what a buyer can't tell from that offer:
>
> 1. **What's included.** "Deep clean" means something different to everyone. List the rooms and the extras (inside oven, baseboards, inside fridge).
> 2. **How big a home.** $250 for a studio and a 4-bedroom reads very differently. Add a size range or tiers.
> 3. **Who it's for.** Move-out? First clean before regular service? Say it.
> 4. **What if they're not happy.** No risk reversal. Something you can keep, like "we come back within 48 hours and fix anything we missed."
> 5. **Why now.** No reason to book this week. Only add one if it's real, like your open slots.
> 6. **What happens next.** Does a deep clean lead into your $140 regular service? Say so, and you have a path, not a one-off.
>
> The one change that matters most: number 1. Show exactly what they get.
>
> Next step: want me to rewrite the offer with these fixed, in three sizes?

*Made-up business for illustration. Your offer review will use your own Business Card.*
