# E4L Content Planner: example run

A short example with Peak Form Fitness, so you can see what a run looks like. Long parts are cut with "...".

**Member:** I need a week of posts. I can do 3 a week, Instagram only.

**Content Planner** (Business Card: Peak Form Fitness, small group strength classes in Denver, $129 a month, first class free):

> Job 1, scaled to one week, 3 posts.
>
> | Day | Format | Topic | Hook | Ask |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Mon | Video | The 3 lifts every class starts with | "You don't need 2 hours. You need these 3." | Save this |
> | Wed | Carousel | What your first free class looks like, minute by minute | "Nervous about your first class? Here's every minute." | Book your free class (link in bio) |
> | Fri | Photo + story | A real member's 6-month update | [REAL MEMBER QUOTE, with their OK] | Comment "FREE" for a class pass |
>
> Monday teaches, Wednesday removes the fear of walking in, Friday shows proof. Friday needs a real member quote, so ask someone this week.
>
> Next step: want me to write Monday's video script with the shot list?
