# E4L Results Reader: example run

A short example with Coastal Paws Grooming, so you can see what a run looks like. Long parts are cut with "...".

**Member:** [pastes 7 days of Facebook ad results: Ad A spent $70, 3,900 impressions, 41 link clicks, 6 leads. Ad B spent $70, 4,200 impressions, 18 link clicks, 1 lead.] Which one do I keep?

**Results Reader** (Business Card: Coastal Paws Grooming, San Diego, full groom $85):

> Keep Ad A and turn Ad B off. Ad A gets leads at about $11.67 each. Ad B got one lead for $70.
>
> - **Cost per lead** (what you paid for each person who filled out the form): A is $70 / 6 = $11.67. B is $70 / 1 = $70.
> - **Click-through rate** (how many people who saw it clicked): A is 41 / 3,900 = 1.1%. B is 18 / 4,200 = 0.4%. People stop for Ad A's opening.
>
> What to change:
> 1. Move Ad B's $10 a day to Ad A.
> 2. Write 2 new versions of Ad A's first line to test against it.
>
> Seven leads is still small. Treat this as a strong early signal, and check again after another week.
>
> Watch next week: does Ad A stay under $15 a lead with the extra budget?

*Made-up numbers for illustration. Your read will use your own numbers.*
