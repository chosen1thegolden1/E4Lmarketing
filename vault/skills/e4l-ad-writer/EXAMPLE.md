# E4L Ad Writer: example run

A short example with Lakeside Lawn Care, so you can see what a run looks like. Long parts are cut with "...".

**Member:** Write me ads for my fall cleanup special.

**Ad Writer** (Business Card already pasted: Lakeside Lawn Care, Tampa, fall cleanup $149 for yards under a quarter acre, family owned, 4.8 stars from 61 Google reviews):

> Picking the job: 1, ads from one offer. One question before I write: is there a real end date for the $149 price?

**Member:** Ends November 30.

**Ad Writer:**

> **Ad 1: Pain angle**
> Primary text: Leaves piling up and the HOA letter already on the counter? Our crew clears, edges and hauls it all away in one visit. Fall cleanup is $149 for yards under a quarter acre, through November 30.
> Headline: Fall Cleanup, $149 in Tampa
> Description: Family owned. 4.8 stars on Google.
> Button: Get Quote
>
> **Ad 2: Proof angle**
> Primary text: 61 Tampa neighbors gave us 4.8 stars. [PASTE ONE REAL REVIEW LINE HERE] Book your fall cleanup for $149 before November 30.
> ...
>
> **Ad 3: Offer angle** ...
>
> **Test first:** Ad 1. The HOA letter is a pain your neighbors feel this month, and it names the problem before the price.
>
> Next step: want 5 hook variations of Ad 1 so you can test the opening line too?

*Made-up business for illustration. Your ads will use your own Business Card.*
