I don't use GoHighLevel. Is the Vault still worth it?
Yes. Every snapshot comes with a "Run it anywhere" playbook: the exact messages, timing and trigger, ready to set up in whatever tool you use, or by hand. GoHighLevel is just the fastest way to run them.

    
Can I use this for my clients?
Yes. Our license lets you use every prompt, skill and snapshot in work for your own clients. You just can't resell the Vault itself or share your login.

It's built for marketing, not everything. It includes GoHighLevel systems nobody else offers, a monthly live call with real people, and a license to use it for your clients.
