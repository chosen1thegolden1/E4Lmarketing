# Client Onboarding — Cloning the Loop

This is how the E4L paid-ads loop becomes a productized service. Prove it on
E4L's own account first; then repeat this checklist per client. The skill is the
same every time — only the connected account and the guardrails change.

## Intake (before touching anything)

Collect and record per client:
- **Goal & KPI:** lead gen (target CPL) or sales (target CPA / target ROAS)?
  Get the *number* — every kill/scale decision keys off it.
- **Economics:** AOV, margin, and max allowable CPA/CPL. Without this you can't
  say what "profitable" means.
- **Budget:** daily/monthly cap and how aggressively they want to scale.
- **Assets:** existing creative, brand guidelines, offers, landing pages.
- **Baseline:** current CPA/ROAS and last 30–90 days of history, so you measure
  lift against *their* baseline, not industry benchmarks.
- **Platforms:** Meta, Google, or both.

## Access & connection

- Connect the ad account via the agreed connector (direct Meta/Google Ads MCP
  preferred for ownership + multi-platform; AdAdvisor acceptable for a fast
  Meta-only start).
- **Request the minimum scope** needed and confirm read vs. write access
  explicitly. Log what was granted.
- Verify you can actually see live data and that conversion tracking
  (Meta CAPI / Google enhanced conversions) is healthy **before** optimizing.
  Bad tracking makes every downstream decision wrong.

## Guardrails (the trust contract)

- **Draft-and-approve by default.** No budget change, pause, or new campaign
  goes live without the client's explicit sign-off, with the reasoning attached.
- Set an **agreed change cadence** (e.g. optimization reviews 2×/week) so you're
  not poking the account daily and resetting learning.
- Define **hard limits** in writing: max daily budget, max single budget jump,
  and "always ask first" actions (new campaigns, audience overhauls, big scales).

## Weekly report template (client-readable)

Same analysis as the internal loop, written for a non-marketer:

```
E4L Paid Media — [Client] — Week of [date]

HEADLINE:   [one line — e.g. "CPL down 22% to $18; scaling the winning hook."]

SPEND / RESULT
  Spend: $X   |   Leads/Sales: N   |   CPA/CPL: $Y   |   ROAS: Z
  vs last week: [▲/▼ with %]     vs goal ($target): [on/under/over]

WHAT WON
  - [Winning ad name] — why it won (the ladder metric that drove it)

WHAT WE KILLED
  - [Ad name(s)] — spent $X, no profitable conversions

DECISIONS THIS WEEK
  - Scaled [ad] $A→$B/day
  - Launched new test: [variable being tested, e.g. 4 new hooks]

NEXT WEEK
  - [Concrete plan: what scales, what gets tested, what to watch]

WATCH ITEMS
  - [e.g. frequency at 2.6 on the winner — creative refresh queued]
```

## Repeatability checklist per client
- [ ] KPI + target number recorded
- [ ] Economics (AOV/margin/max CPA) recorded
- [ ] Account connected, access scope logged
- [ ] Conversion tracking verified healthy
- [ ] Naming convention applied
- [ ] Guardrails & cadence agreed in writing
- [ ] Baseline captured for lift measurement
- [ ] First test structured (one variable, 3–5 variants, budget ≥ target CPA)
- [ ] Weekly report scheduled
