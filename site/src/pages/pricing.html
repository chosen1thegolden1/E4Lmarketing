The rules

Seven things that are always true.

Systems are a flat monthly fee.

An AI Front Desk, a quiz funnel, a chatbot: same price whether it handles 10 leads or 10,000. That's the point of a system.

Creative is priced by output.

Content Engine plans are sized by how many ads, cuts, and graphics get made each month. You pick the tier. Moving up or down takes effect next cycle, with no fee and no re-onboarding.

Expansion services are one flat price each.

Ads management, AI search visibility, email and text workflows, voice AI, chatbot, content management, influencer management, website redesign. Add any of them, drop any of them.

We never bill in credits.

Some AI companies sell you "credits" and you find out later what one is worth. We don't. You see dollars, minutes, and messages. If a bill ever confuses you, that's our mistake to fix.

Usage beyond your plan is billed at cost.

Voice minutes, text messages, and platform fees for the AI agents are passed through as your volume grows. At cost. No markup hidden in a unit you can't check.

Ad spend and creator fees are yours, not ours.

Your media budget goes straight to Meta, Google, or TikTok. Influencer payments go to the creator. Our fee covers the management, never the spend.

No results are promised. Numbers are reported.

Any estimate you see from us (including the Scorecard) is built from industry benchmarks. Every month you get what actually happened, in dollars and hours.

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. No minimum term dressed up as a discount. Systems keep running as long as you want them to.

What if I only need one thing?

Then buy one thing. Most businesses start with the system that seals their biggest leak, usually the Front Desk, and add from there.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Your phone numbers, your customer list, your ad accounts, your content. Leave any time and take it all.

Do I have to learn anything?

No. You approve scripts and key visuals for creative, and you read a monthly report. Everything else is ours.

Do you work with my industry?

We're built for local service businesses: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, dental, med spa, law, vet, salons, and the like. If you're a national chain, we'll say so on the call.

Can I see it before I buy it?

Yes. Ask for a personalized voice AI demo on the call. We'll build one that answers as your business.
