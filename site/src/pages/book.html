The Game Plan Call

20 minutes. No pitch deck.

We look at where money is leaking out of your business and whether we can plug it. If we can't, we'll tell you, and you keep the map.

What happens on the call
  • We pull up your numbers. If you ran the Scorecard, we start there. If not, we'll do it live in two minutes.
  • We find the biggest leak. Missed calls, slow follow-up, a cold list, or nobody seeing you. Usually one is bleeding more than the rest.
  • We map the fix. Which system seals it, in what order, and what it costs. Straight answers on price, in dollars.
  • You decide. No follow-up pressure. The map is yours either way.
