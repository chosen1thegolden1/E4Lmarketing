## How to Decide What's Important

### 🔴 SURFACE NOW (high priority — Sebastian needs to see this)
Flag if ANY of these are true:
- **Money:** invoices, payments, receipts owed/due, contracts, pricing,
  refunds, chargebacks, revenue. Anything financial.
- **Client needs:** an active or prospective client asking for help, raising
  a concern, or waiting on a response — especially for the new AI service.
- **Sign-ups / onboarding:** new client signing up or being onboarded to the
  AI service offering.
- **Deadlines:** anything time-sensitive with a due date or "by [date]" ask.
- **Compliance / legal:** anything from @mcandjlaw.com, trademark/USPTO,
  legal filings, regulatory, or compliance matters.
- **VIP sender:** email from Rozel, Wilbert, Sean, or the paralegal — or any
  @gsgagency.com / @mcandjlaw.com address.
- **Needs Sebastian personally:** a query only he can answer / decide, that
  cannot be delegated to Rozel or the team.

### 🟡 CAN WAIT / DELEGATE
- Routine internal coordination that Rozel or a team member can handle.
- FYIs, meeting recaps, and recordings (Fireflies, Zoom) — useful but not
  urgent unless they contain an action item for me.

### ⚪ LOW PRIORITY / IGNORE (noise)
- **Promotions & offers** — including lists I can't seem to unsubscribe from.
- **Marketing/promotional email from services I use** (product blasts,
  "new feature" upsells, webinars I'm not running).
- **Newsletters** (e.g. The Neuron, Substack digests, side-hustle/AI-trend
  mailers) — read on my own time, never surface as urgent.
- **Automated billing/notification summaries** (e.g. Upwork weekly billing)
  unless they signal a problem (payment failed, dispute, action required).

### Judgment rules
- When money, a client, a deadline, or compliance is involved, **lean toward
  surfacing** even if the sender is unknown.
- When it's promotional or purely informational, **lean toward muting.**
- If something looks delegable to Rozel/team, note that in the summary rather
  than treating it as a personal must-do.
