Related questions

What counts as speed-to-lead automation?

It's the system that responds the moment a lead comes in — a missed call, a form fill, a text — so the business follows up before the lead calls a competitor. We set it up to trigger a call, text, or email automatically.

Do I need an AI voice receptionist if I already have automation for missed calls?

Not necessarily. Missed-call automation covers follow-up after the fact; an AI voice receptionist answers the call itself, live, 24/7, and can book the appointment on the spot. Some businesses use one, some use both.

Can I test the system before paying for it?

For the voice receptionist, yes — we build a demo from your real website and give you a live phone number to call and test before you buy anything.
