# Eat 4 Life Marketing — Content Intake

## Who we are
Eat 4 Life Marketing is an AI marketing agency serving local service businesses — med spas, home-service trades (roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC), dental practices, salons, gyms, restaurants, and other owner-operated businesses. Tagline: **AI Marketing Made Easy.** Based in Los Angeles, CA, working with clients anywhere in the US.

## What we do (services)
- **AI voice receptionists** — a phone agent trained on the client's own business that answers every call 24/7, qualifies the caller (name, number, job, urgency), and books the appointment or estimate. Built per-business, with a live demo line prospects can call before buying.
- **Missed-call and speed-to-lead automation** — every inquiry gets an immediate follow-up (call, text, email) so leads stop leaking to whoever answers first.
- **AI Search Visibility (GEO)** — a monthly service that measures whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity recommend the client when customers ask who to call, then publishes the structured answer content that makes the client findable, readable, and trustworthy to AI. Monthly before/after reporting with screenshots.
- **AI-readable website builds** — sites shipped with schema markup and FAQ structure by default, so AI assistants can read and cite them.
- **Revenue-leak diagnostics** — a short assessment that shows an owner where inquiries, follow-up, and no-shows are costing them revenue each month.

## How we work
Systems over hustle: we build automated pipelines that run without the owner's involvement, then report results monthly in plain language with evidence (screenshots, before/after numbers). Everything we ship is tested end to end before a client sees it.

## Proof points (safe to cite)
- We run our own AI Search Visibility audits on ourselves monthly and publish the baseline — we practice the exact system we sell.
- Our voice-agent demos are built from a prospect's real website and connected to a live phone number, so owners can call and test the agent before paying anything.

## What we never say
- No promised rankings, citations, or revenue results — we promise the work and the positioning; results are reported, not guaranteed.
- No fabricated reviews or statistics.

## Tone
Plain, direct, confident. Short sentences. No hype words, no exclamation points. Talk like an operator, not an ad.
