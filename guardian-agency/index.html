Our specialty

Stop renting money from the bank. Become the bank.

It's called the Infinite Banking Concept. You over-fund a permanent life policy, usually an indexed universal life (IUL) or dividend-paying whole life, and use the cash value as your own lending source.

  • Borrow without a credit check. Take a policy loan for a car, a down payment, tuition, or a business, on your terms.
  • Your money keeps working. Your full cash value keeps earning even while you have a loan out.
  • Tax-advantaged access. Policy loans are generally not taxed as income when structured correctly.
  • The protection never leaves. Your family is still covered by the death benefit the whole time.
See if I qualify

Cash value growth in an IUL is tied to a market index with a floor and cap and is not a direct investment. Loans reduce the death benefit until repaid. We'll show you a carrier illustration before you commit to anything.

Questions people ask

Straight answers.

Is “infinite banking” a real thing or a gimmick?

It's a real strategy used for decades by families and business owners, built on permanent life insurance with cash value. It is not magic and it is not for everyone. It works best when you can fund a policy consistently for 7+ years. On our call we'll run an actual carrier illustration so you see real numbers, not hype.

What's the difference between IUL and whole life for this?

Whole life offers guaranteed cash value growth plus potential dividends. Indexed universal life (IUL) credits interest based on a market index with a 0% floor and a cap, which can grow faster but with less guarantee. We'll show you both and recommend based on your goals and risk comfort.

Do I need a medical exam?

Often, no. Many carriers now offer accelerated underwriting for healthy applicants up to $1M or more. If an exam is needed, a nurse comes to you and it takes about 20 minutes.

How do you get paid?

By the insurance carrier when a policy is issued. You never pay us a fee, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or not.

Can you help with Medicare if I'm turning 65 soon?

Yes. Your initial enrollment window opens three months before your 65th birthday. Reach out early and we'll map out Parts A, B, D and whether an Advantage or Supplement plan fits you best.
