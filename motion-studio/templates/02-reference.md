# Give it a reference (guide step 5)

Without a reference, Opus falls back to its defaults. Name or show a style; don't describe one.
Good sources: launch videos from companies you admire, Dribbble's motion section, competitors' launch films,
or a folder of E4L's own past work (nobody else can copy that one).

```
Reference: films/[name]/refs/launch.mp4

1. Extract one frame every 0.5s with ffmpeg. Study them.
2. Write films/[name]/docs/style_guide.md: palette (hex), type (family, weight, spacing),
   shot lengths, transition types, camera moves, texture, how text enters and exits.
3. Write films/[name]/docs/shotlist.md for a [DURATION]s video about [SUBJECT] in THAT style.
   Take the grammar of the reference, never its content, logos, or characters.
4. Show me both files. Wait for my OK before any code.
```

For a single frame: "Take the colors, type and texture from refs/frame.png. Do not take the subject."
