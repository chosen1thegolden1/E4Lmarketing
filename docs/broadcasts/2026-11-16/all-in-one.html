Does Google know you're closed Thursday?

Preview One setting. A couple of minutes. Before the holiday week.

Sells Game Plan Call (agency front end)

Hey {{contact.first_name}},

Thanksgiving is next Thursday, Nov 26.

Quick one before the holiday week.

Pull out your phone and search for your own business on Google.

Look at the hours. 👀

If you're closed Thursday, or opening late, or leaving early the day after, does Google know that?

Google has a setting for exactly this. It's called Special hours.

Google's own help page says it's for holidays, and it recommends confirming your hours for official holidays "even if they're the same as your regular hours."

Here's the whole fix:

1. Open your Google Business Profile and choose Edit profile, then Hours.
2. Click the pencil next to "Special hours."
3. Add the date. Uncheck "Closed" to set your hours, or leave it checked for a closed day.
4. Save.

Google says special hours show to customers only on the days you set.

That takes a couple of minutes. Do it this weekend and it's handled.

Hours tell people when you're closed. They don't stop the phone from ringing.
