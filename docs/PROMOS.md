# Promo decisions — Chosen's word only

A seasonal email can only carry a deadline if something really ends. The calendar ends by
itself. **A sale ends only if it is written here.**

The Tuesday writer and the editor both read this file. If a week has a commercial holiday
in it and no live row here, the batch ships **without** a promo and without a deadline, and
the writer asks Chosen rather than inventing one.

**Any row under "Decisions needed" whose date is 14 days out or closer gets raised with
Chosen in the writer's final message, every week, until he answers or the date passes.**

## What a promo needs before it counts as live

From `jfsi.md`: a **reason why** it exists and why it ends, **one offer** (not a menu), and
a **real deadline** that gets kept. Plus the list it's for, and a working checkout URL —
an offer with no link is an email that cannot convert.

### ⚠️ THE PROMO SPANS TWO BATCHES. Neither can miss it.
Computed, not assumed:

| Batch | Written | Sends | Carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week of **Nov 23** | Tue **Nov 17** | Mon 23 · Wed 25 · **Fri 27 (Black Friday)** · Sun 29 | the open, the Thanksgiving-week build-up, the Sunday push |
| Week of **Nov 30** | Tue **Nov 24** | **Mon 30 (Cyber Monday)** · Wed Dec 2 · Fri Dec 4 · Sun Dec 6 | **the close** |

Cyber Monday is the first day of the *following* week. A writer who only treats Nov 23 as
the promo week will write the open and never write the close. Per `jfsi.md`, the close is
where the money is: last-day FAQ, then a last-hours reminder to recent openers only.

### Open questions for Chosen — answer before Tue Nov 17
1. **The window.** Confirm Fri Nov 27 to Mon Nov 30, or give the real dates.
2. **Is the mini course $50, or $50 off?** Written as "$50" here because that is the
   plainest reading of "mini course they can get $50". Say which.
3. **Is the mini course only for book buyers, or can anyone buy it at $50?**
4. **The $2,000.** For "usually $2k" to be printable, the course has to have genuinely
   sold at $2,000. If it hasn't, the comparison can't be used — a struck-through price
   that was never charged is the one marketing claim that can actually get a business in
   trouble, and this list would smell it anyway. If $2,000 is real, say so and it stays.
5. **Does the agency list get anything?** Both named offers are student products. As it
   stands the agency four carry **no promo** that week, which is a defensible choice —
   owners are running their own promos, not reading ours. Confirm or give an agency offer.
6. **One offer, or two?** `jfsi.md` says one offer per promo, not a menu. Two prices in
   one email splits the decision and lowers both. Recommended: the **course at $500** is
   the headline on the big sends, and the **book + free audiobook** carries the Sunday and
   the softer slots as the low-ticket entry. Confirm or reorder.
