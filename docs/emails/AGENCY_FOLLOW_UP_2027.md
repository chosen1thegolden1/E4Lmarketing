## What the editor has to handle (in this order)

1. **Greeting:** `{Fname}` becomes `Hey [First Name],`. That's the agency wall, not a
   style choice. The mid-email `(fname)` becomes `[First Name]`.
2. **Typos, and nothing else in those lines:** "most true sentence" → "truest sentence"
   (or "most true sentences"), "your in" → "you're in", "asking  people" (double space),
   "theseeads" → "these leads", "27'" → "'27".
3. **Subject and preview.** Chosen labeled them *Headline* and *Subject*. The headline,
   "Are you ready to level up in 2027?", is the stronger line, so it's probably the
   subject, with his "Subject" line as preview text. "Well read this clearly...." most
   likely means "Well, read this closely…". Subject lines are the editor's job anyway, so
   it runs `sauce.md`'s read-it-out-loud test on both and **tells Chosen which way round
   it put them.** No "Happy New Year" subject, which is a hard wall.
4. **The open quote:** "The fortune is in the follow up" needs its closing period or
   question mark. Keep the quote marks.
5. **"Going out of business notice"**: the quotes should wrap the sign, not the sentence:
   *a "Going Out of Business" notice on your door or website.*
6. **Emoji:** 🤔😳 is his and stays. The agency allowance is 2–3, so add the 👉 on the
   CTA and that's the budget. Nothing holiday-themed. 🎉🥂 is decoration and a wall.
7. **Bold:** one or two. "cracks cause leaks…nasty revenue leaks" is the line a skimmer
   should catch.
8. **Two links, both his call.** The site: `https://e4lagency.com/` with
   `utm_campaign=A2-2026-12-28`. The Game Plan Call:
   `https://api.leadconnectorhq.com/widget/bookings/cs-game-plan-call`, same UTM. One
   CTA per email is the house rule, but Chosen asked for both, and they land on the same
   decision, so both stay. 👉 goes on the call line.
9. **Sign-off:** add `Sebastian / Eat 4 Life / AI Marketing Made Easy`. He didn't write
   one.
