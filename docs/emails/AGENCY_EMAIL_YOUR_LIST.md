## ⚠️ The numbers: Chosen's original line vs. what the screenshot shows

Chosen wrote:

> For context: She did 33k that month but all she did was offer discounts, sales and
> services in her email and her sales went ka-boom. Jumped to 47K for the month. A 14k+
> increase due to one channel... Email.

**The screenshot doesn't support 33K → 47K.** It shows $14,960 from email and says that was
**33%** of her store's revenue, so the month was about **$45,300**, not $47K. "33k" looks like
the **33%** misread as a dollar figure. It also shows email revenue *more than doubled* vs.
August (">100%"), but not her total for any month, so "jumped from X to Y" can't be read
off it.

**So the copy below uses only what's on the screenshot**, and says it in his voice. If
Chosen has the real store totals (from her Shopify, say) and they really were 33K → 47K,
he says so and his line goes back in. **Nobody ships 33K/47K without that.** An email
whose screenshot contradicts its own sentence is one a reader with a calculator catches,
and this list is business owners.
