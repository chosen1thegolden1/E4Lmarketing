a real E4L price and a season hook, so the slot never runs dry. The rows rotate across
three tracks (business, gamer, creator) so every reader sees their own path at least
monthly.

promo in `docs/PROMOS.md` (the row becomes "what you'll build in the course"), or a major
tool launch (the row becomes "this just dropped," and the displaced row slides a week).

(Creative Packs, one-time) · Pages $500/mo (Quiz Funnel Pages) · Front Desk $500/mo (AI
Front Desk) · Video $497/mo (The Content Engine). Long-weekend Mondays move to Wednesday,
per `seasons.md`.
