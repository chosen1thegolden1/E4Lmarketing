# Student list: two lanes, nobody removed (Chosen, 2026-11-04)

**Why:** about 1,880 of ~2,000 students skip each weekday email. Sending to people who never
open teaches Gmail and Outlook that the emails aren't wanted, which pushes them toward spam
for the readers too. Chosen's concern about a "do you still want these?" cut: *"im scared if
no one says yes. Our whole list will be tiny, more honest. but tiny."* So nobody is cut.

## The plan
1. **Readers lane:** contacts who opened or clicked any email in the last 90 days get all four
   weekly emails, same as now.
2. **Quiet lane:** everyone else stays on the list and gets **one email a month**, the best
   performer we have (Sunday personal stories have led on both lists). Anyone who opens a
   monthly email moves back to the readers lane automatically.
3. **No "still want these?" email.**
4. **Black Friday 2026 goes to the whole list.** A real sale with a real deadline is the best
   re-engagement there is. Openers move into the readers lane.
5. **Revisit after Black Friday** with real numbers.
