## For Dara — why this works, and how to make more

### The lever
Don't have Malik explain AI. Drop him inside the owner's worst 30 seconds, then
make him the one who catches the call. Humor comes from the situation (hands
full, phone just out of reach) — never from making the owner look dumb.

### The 7-beat formula (reusable)
1. **Hook** — the owner physically can't reach the phone (specific, visual, a little funny).
2. **Stakes** — what that one call is worth (always hedge with "industry estimate").
3. **The knife** — nobody leaves voicemails; they call the next guy.
4. **The pile-up** — a comedic end-of-day tally.
5. **The turn** — Malik handles it; his line is "I got this one."
6. **The after** — booked while you worked.
7. **CTA** — the free Revenue Leak Scorecard.

### 5 scenario swaps (same skeleton, new trade)
- **Plumber** — both arms under the sink, water running.
- **Barber** — mid-fade, can't stop or he ruins it.
- **Caterer** — both hands in a hot tray during the dinner rush.
- **Salon owner** — foils in a client's hair, timer running.
- **HVAC tech** — in a crawlspace, phone up in the van.

### Guardrails (from the Creative Pack)
- Plain English, no slang, no emojis in the slides.
- Every number says "estimate."
- Dollars, never credits.
- CTA is always the Scorecard.
- Tagline "AI Marketing Made Easy."
- Keep the AI-disclosure line on the CTA slide.
- Malik points at what he's talking about.
