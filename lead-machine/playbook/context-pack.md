## What E4L sells (the needs the bot qualifies for)
1. AI voice receptionist — trained on the prospect's own business; answers every call 24/7, qualifies the caller (name, number, job, urgency), books the appointment/estimate. E4L builds a LIVE DEMO from the prospect's real website and gives them a phone number to call before they pay. Go-live = the client sets one conditional call-forwarding rule for missed/busy calls; rollback is one dial code.
2. Missed-call text-back + speed-to-lead automation — every missed call / form / DM / text gets an automated response in under 60 seconds and follow-up until booked. Includes database reactivation of old lead lists.
3. AI Search Visibility (GEO) — monthly: asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity (and Claude) the vertical's 7 "money questions" (e.g. "emergency roofer near Coral Springs, FL", "roofer that answers the phone Coral Springs, FL", "best-reviewed roofer near me"), screenshots who gets recommended, then publishes structured answer pages + FAQ schema. A FREE prospect audit ("Named in 0 of 28 AI answers — here are the competitors AI recommends instead") is the universal cold open.
4. AI-readable website builds / redesign (schema + FAQ structure by default).
5. Quiz funnels, chatbot, content engine (AI video ads / social cuts), ads management, review engine, email/SMS workflows — the expansion menu.
6. Revenue Leak Scorecard — free 2-minute quiz estimating money leaking from missed calls, no-shows, dead follow-up. Link: https://e4lmarketingdemos.com/revenue-leak-scorecard-page
7. Game Plan Call — free 20-minute call with Sebastian. Link: https://api.leadconnectorhq.com/widget/bookings/cs-game-plan-call

## Cold-call hooks already in use (voice to match)
- Missed-call angle: "about half the calls to businesses like yours ring out or hit voicemail — and that homeowner just calls the next name on Google. We install an AI that answers every one of those calls, books the estimate, and texts back every missed call in under a minute. It runs 24/7 and it never calls in sick."
- AI-search angle: "when's the last time you asked ChatGPT who the best roofer in [city] is? We run that exact check. Most owners find out their competitors come up and they don't."
- Qualify questions: "Who answers your phones after hours right now — you, voicemail, or nobody?" / "Roughly how many calls or leads a week are you working with?"
- Proof lever: "we build a demo line trained on your own company you can call and talk to before you ever pay a dollar."
- Every call ends with ask #1 (Game Plan Call) or ask #2 (email for the free AI visibility audit).

## Email voice rules (from the e4l-intro-email skill and draft.js)
- Direct, warm, specific, human; East Coast confident, not slangy. Never corporate.
- Never "I hope this email finds you well", "just checking in", "circling back", "quick question about".
- No hype words: revolutionary, cutting-edge, unlock, leverage, game-changer, seamless.
- Don't lead with "AI"; lead with their customers and revenue.
- Never promise specific revenue numbers or results. Body under 150 words (cold: 55–90 words).
- Opening sentence must reference something concrete about THEIR business.
- Cold emails from the CS account go plain-text with NO links until deliverability warm-up clears.

## Hard guardrails for the bot
- Public, citable sources only. Never fabricate or pattern-guess an email, name, phone, or fact. Blank + "not found" beats a guess.
- Never promise results, rankings, citations, or revenue in any suggested copy.
- Compliance flags: medical (no promised results, minimal health detail), legal (no legal advice/outcomes), fair housing (no demographic steering), auto (no price/APR/trade-in commitments).
- Nothing the bot produces creates a task for Chosen; it delivers a reviewed-ready sheet and a short summary.
